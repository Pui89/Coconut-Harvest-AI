# Free-First Marketing, SEO and Sales Playbook

## Purpose
Help a prospective farm, grower, agricultural company or robotics partner understand the Coconut Harvest AI concept and request a discussion or pilot. This is a product-discovery plan, not a claim of validated field performance.

## Positioning
**AI-assisted coconut detection and harvest planning for future robotic harvesting.**

Describe the current state accurately:
- Separate implemented software, prototype, simulation, and planned hardware.
- No validated physical harvesting robot or measured real-world performance is claimed unless supported by published test evidence.
- Tree heights up to 30 m are a design challenge/target, not a verified reach capability.
- Do not promise yield, labor savings, safety, speed, or accuracy without measured evidence.

## Free-first channel plan
1. **Product page:** explain the problem, workflow, current status, limitations, and how to contact Pui89.
2. **Visual demo:** use a short, clearly labelled walkthrough of the current software/prototype. LiveDemo's free tier currently supports up to three published demos, but includes a watermark; do not upload private farm/customer data. Check current terms before use.
3. **Search visibility:** use a clear page title, one descriptive H1, plain-language headings, image alt text, descriptive repository links, and a concise FAQ. Audit the public page with a free scanner such as FreeScan.app, then manually verify important findings.
4. **Proof-led content:** publish brief progress notes showing what was tested, test conditions, limitations, and results. Separate measured results from planned targets.
5. **Direct outreach:** begin with relevant local growers, agricultural equipment companies, cooperatives, and research/engineering partners. Personalise messages; do not scrape personal data or send bulk unsolicited messages.
6. **Feedback loop:** ask a small number of target users what harvest task is costly, what evidence they would need, and what a useful pilot would look like. Record consent and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Suggested first customer-facing page
- Problem: coconut harvest planning and access can be difficult across varied palms and farm layouts.
- Concept: AI-assisted visual detection and harvest selection, with a future robotic harvesting workflow.
- Workflow: image input → candidate detection/assessment → human review → planned robotic action (conceptual until validated).
- Current status: link to implemented code/tests and state what remains under development.
- Evidence: show only traceable images, licences/credits, test protocol, and actual metrics once available.
- Call to action: “Discuss a farm workflow or pilot” with a contact method controlled by the project owner.

## SEO checklist
- Use terms real customers might search for, naturally: coconut harvesting technology, coconut detection, agricultural robotics, AI-assisted harvest planning.
- Give every public image meaningful alt text and verify permission to publish it.
- Add a short project summary, public contact route, and links to the source repository.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, fake reviews, fabricated customer logos, or claims of being the best/most accurate.
- Recheck page links, mobile layout, page title, description, and image loading before sharing.

## Sales qualification questions
1. What crop, farm layout, and harvesting workflow do you use now?
2. What is the main problem: identifying harvest-ready fruit, reaching palms, worker availability, cost, or another issue?
3. What would a safe, useful first trial need to demonstrate?
4. Who would evaluate the trial and what evidence would they require?
5. Is there a suitable site and permission to collect test images/data?

## Free-first tools and cautions
- **FreeScan.app:** use for an initial public-page audit; its paid monitoring features are optional.
- **LiveDemo:** optional; free tier has a published-demo limit and watermark. A simple video or GIF in the repository is also acceptable.
- **NotFair / paid ad tools:** do not connect an ad account or launch ads until there is a clear offer, landing page, budget approval, and conversion measurement.
- **Social schedulers:** do not add hosted subscriptions just to publish occasional progress updates; manual posting is free. Self-hosted software still has setup, maintenance, and infrastructure costs.
- Never add paid API keys, cloud GPU, paid hosting, or ad spend without explicit approval.

## Definition of done
- A visitor can identify the problem, current capability, limitations, and next step in under two minutes.
- Every technical claim is backed by code, a reproducible test, or is clearly marked as planned.
- All public images have verified provenance and permission.
- The contact route works and does not expose private information.
- No paid service or external account is enabled without approval.
