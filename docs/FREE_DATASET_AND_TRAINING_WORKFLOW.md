# Coconut image evidence and dataset preparation

## Current decision

Detector training and inference are disabled in this repository. No detector framework or model checkpoint is selected, and no coconut detection accuracy is claimed.

## Image evidence

- Collect orchard photographs only with permission or documented reuse rights.
- Record source, licence, attribution, capture date/session and file checksum.
- Keep private farm images outside the public repository.
- Use human review for image quality, fruit visibility and annotations.
- Split related images by tree, orchard or capture session to reduce leakage.

The repository's offline annotator, format converter and dataset audit utilities may be used for data preparation. They do not train a model, prove annotation quality, establish legal rights or measure detection performance.

## Current evidence status

- Detector implementation: retired.
- Detector training/evaluation: disabled.
- Trained coconut weights: not included or claimed to exist.
- Coconut detection metrics: none claimed.
- Application: manual-evidence and simulation workflows only.

Any future model proposal requires a separate approval and full review of code, weights, dependencies, licences, security, dataset quality and held-out evaluation evidence.
