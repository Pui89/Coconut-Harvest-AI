## Interpretation and limitations

1. The licence statements above were visible on the corresponding Commons pages when reviewed on 14 November 2026. Recheck the exact file revision, licence URL, creator and attribution immediately before any reuse or acquisition.
2. A source-page licence is not a legal opinion and does not by itself establish that every depicted element is rights-cleared.
3. Source-page descriptions and captions are not independent biological maturity labels.
4. No candidate has passed PUI89's full human-review checklist. No candidate is approved for annotation or dataset inclusion by this audit.
5. The page previews and metadata were inspected; the original image bytes were not acquired or hash-verified, and no native-resolution visual quality assessment was performed.
6. Keep orchard-attached fruit and harvested/plucked fruit in separate task contexts. Do not force the four operational classes from husk colour alone.
