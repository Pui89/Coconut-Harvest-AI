# Coconut Attached-Green-Fruit Source Candidate Supplement

**Review date:** 14 November 2026  
**Status:** metadata-only research shortlist; not an approved dataset.  
**Scope:** additional Wikimedia Commons source pages that appear relevant to attached green coconut fruit, canopy context or general fruiting-palm context.

## Evidence boundary

This is a source-page shortlist based on Commons page metadata and displayed licence statements. It is not a native-resolution image-quality review. The original image bytes have not been acquired, locally hashed or independently inspected in this work. No image is approved for annotation, dataset inclusion or model training.

A green husk does not distinguish PUI89's `young_green` from `mature_green`. Do not assign either class from colour, apparent size, title, or uploader caption alone. Keep biological maturity, visible husk colour, cultivar and attachment/context as separate fields. Expert review is required.

## Source-page checks and caveats

- WMC-06 page displayed CC BY 4.0 and the author attribution; the page reports the original dimensions and file size.
- WMC-07 page displayed CC BY-SA 4.0; structured metadata reports 3,072 × 4,096 pixels and a SHA-1 value. That remote metadata is not a local SHA-256 integrity check.
- WMC-08 and WMC-09 pages displayed CC BY-SA 4.0. WMC-09 page also exposes a SHA-1 value; it is not a substitute for hashing acquired original bytes.
- WMC-10 page displayed CC BY-SA 3.0 and reports EXIF date context.
- WMC-11 page displayed CC0 1.0.
- WMC-12 page displayed CC BY-SA 4.0 and reports image dimensions plus a SHA-1 value.
- Recheck the exact file revision, creator, licence and attribution immediately before acquisition or reuse. Commons metadata and uploader declarations are source evidence, not a legal opinion or an independent guarantee of every depicted element's rights status.

## Candidate handling recommendation

1. Prioritise native-resolution human review of WMC-06 and WMC-07 for attached-fruit visibility and image quality.
2. Review WMC-11 as a useful CC0 candidate if its visible fruit and canopy context meet the task.
3. Keep WMC-08 to WMC-10 and WMC-12 as secondary candidates until their actual visual content is checked.
4. Do not label any of these as `young_green` or `mature_green` without cultivar/development evidence and qualified review.
5. If approved for acquisition, download only after operator approval, preserve original bytes, compute local SHA-256, and complete the human review checklist before annotation or dataset promotion.
