# PUI89 Research, Competition and Commercial Evidence

## PhD application

Present the three robots as experimental platforms for:

**Reliable Multimodal Embodied AI for Safety-Critical Autonomous Robots**

Core research themes:
- multimodal 3D world models;
- uncertainty-aware reasoning;
- OOD detection and safe abstention;
- foundation-model task planning;
- deterministic safety supervision;
- cross-environment transfer;
- simulation-to-real evaluation.

## Robotics competitions

Every competition demo should show a measurable mission:

`Perceive → Localize → Plan → Safety-check → Act → Verify`

Report:
- mission success;
- time;
- energy;
- perception metrics;
- planning metrics;
- failures;
- recovery;
- human interventions.

A repeatable autonomous mission is more valuable than a static concept render.

## Investors

Investor evidence should emphasize:
- painful customer problem;
- measurable economic benefit;
- defensible data;
- reusable robotics platform;
- recurring software revenue;
- fleet economics;
- hardware/service margin;
- safety and deployment strategy;
- expansion from one vertical into multiple verticals.

## Customers

Customer pilots should define:
- baseline workflow;
- target KPI;
- test environment;
- acceptance criteria;
- safety boundary;
- data ownership;
- support plan;
- pilot-to-production criteria.

## Evidence ladder

```Concept
  ↓
Simulation
  ↓
Lab prototype
  ↓
Controlled field test
  ↓
Repeated field validation
  ↓
Paid pilot
  ↓
Production deployment
  ↓
Fleet scale
```

Do not skip levels in public claims.
